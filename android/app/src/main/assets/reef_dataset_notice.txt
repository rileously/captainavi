Captain Avi bundled reef-boundary awareness dataset

Source: OneMap Reef Dataset, Maldives Land and Survey Authority

The source polygons were transformed from WGS 84 / UTM zone 43N (EPSG:32643)
to WGS 84 longitude/latitude and quantized to one-millionth of a degree. Polygon
topology, including the source dataset's holes and multipart features, is retained.

These reef boundaries are for situational awareness only. They contain no surveyed
soundings, safety contours, chart datum, under-keel clearance, or Notices to
Mariners corrections and must not be used as a substitute for official nautical charts.
